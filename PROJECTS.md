# EDDY BUILDS — Project Portfolio

A collection of projects, prototypes, and early-stage ideas exploring artificial intelligence, software development, and real-world problem solving.

## 1. LegacySense AI

**Area:** Industrial AI and energy intelligence  
**Current stage:** Presentation and UI/dashboard mockup

### Problem
Industrial environments may need better visibility into energy use and operational patterns, including when existing equipment cannot easily be replaced.

### Approach
Exploring an energy-intelligence interface for understanding operational data and presenting useful insights.

### Current progress
The current work is at the presentation and UI/dashboard mockup stage. Measured energy savings, industrial deployment, and validated performance are not claimed.

---

## 2. ArogyaEdge

**Area:** Healthcare AI  
**Current stage:** Concept and solution exploration

### Problem
Resource-limited healthcare settings can face challenges with documentation, connectivity, and access to digital assistance.

### Approach
Exploring an offline-first clinical assistance workspace with an emphasis on privacy, local processing, and human oversight.

### Important limitation
This concept is not a replacement for qualified clinicians. Clinical safety and real-world effectiveness require appropriate validation.

---

## 3. RoadGuard AI

**Area:** Road safety and AI  
**Current stage:** Prototype development

### Problem
Road users and safety teams need practical ways to identify and understand potential road risks.

### Approach
Exploring an AI-assisted road-safety prototype.

### Current progress
The prototype is under development. Its performance and real-world road-safety impact have not been established.

---

## 4. FixTrack

**Area:** Campus software and complaint tracking  
**Current stage:** Academic project in development

### Problem
Campus complaints can be difficult to track consistently when issue details and status updates are not easy to follow.

### Approach
Developing a complaint-tracking workflow to organise complaint records, priorities, and status information.

### Current progress
The project is being developed and tested. Completion and reliable end-to-end operation should be confirmed through testing before being claimed.

---

## 5. AyurQC AI

**Area:** AI-assisted quality screening  
**Current stage:** Early-stage idea

### Problem
Quality-control teams may need support identifying unusual raw-material batches for further investigation.

### Approach
Exploring whether AI-assisted screening could help flag material that requires additional review.

### Important limitation
An AI flag alone cannot establish authenticity, purity, adulteration, or medicinal safety. Laboratory testing and qualified assessment remain essential.

---

## 6. UniCampus

**Area:** Education technology and campus systems  
**Current stage:** Platform concept

### Problem
Academic information, campus workflows, student support, and communications can be spread across separate systems.

### Approach
Exploring a unified campus platform concept with role-based access and student-support workflows.

### Design principle
Student privacy, appropriate permissions, transparency, and human oversight should guide the platform's design.

---

## 7. Continuity AI

**Area:** Healthcare information continuity  
**Current stage:** Concept

### Problem
Important care information can become fragmented when patients move between healthcare providers or care settings.

### Approach
Exploring ways to help authorised people maintain access to relevant care context across transitions.

### Design principle
Consent, access controls, privacy, and information accuracy are central requirements. The concept is not presented as a deployed clinical system.

---

## Development principles

Across these projects, my focus is on:

- Understanding the problem before choosing a technology.
- Building and testing incrementally.
- Being transparent about project progress and limitations.
- Considering privacy, safety, accessibility, and human oversight.
- Learning from feedback and improving through iteration.
